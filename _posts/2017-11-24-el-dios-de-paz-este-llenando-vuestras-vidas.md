May the God of peace fill your lives and ministries.

It is a joy for the EPYL ministry to have your valuable friendship and fellowship.

Beloved brothers, after many plans in the Lord, our ministry work has intensified, and we are strengthened by the strength that comes from him.

Of our work among the pastors and leaders, we let you know what God is doing through us.

- The group of pastors and leaders of Totoray, Curahuasi, finished the third course of this year. This time we had as guest teacher Pastor Vaylete Velásquez, teaching New Testament survey.

[[foto1]]

- On Monday, November 27, in the early morning we leave for Tintec, Paucartambo. The guest teacher for this training is Pastor Juan David Quispe Aquino. The course to be taught is New Testament survey. Pastor Felipe Cjanahuire will also accompany us. Pastor Felipe will preach in Quechua at night to the congregation of the church in Tintec. I must mention that Pastor Felipe was trained through the EPYL ministry program.

- On this trip brothers Reynaldo and Remigio will accompany us, to train in the knowledge of the Word and in the ministry among the Quechua.

Our arrival in Totoray, and the whole group of brothers who took part in the training. There is much potential in this church for leaders. We ask for your prayers for them.
