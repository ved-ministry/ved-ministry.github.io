> Luke 24:26 – Was it not necessary that the Christ should suffer these things and enter into his glory?

Two disciples were going on the first day of the week to Emmaus, talking about what had happened in Jerusalem. Today many go out walking and traveling on days off work; to what end? It is possible to have Bible walks, to talk about what happened in biblical times. Who is Christ? Did Christ know what would happen to him?

## His sufferings

The prophets declared everything concerning Christ: his life, miracles, and his sufferings at the hands of the elders and rulers. Luke 24:44 Then he said to them, “These are my words that I spoke to you while I was still with you, that everything written about me in the Law of Moses and the Prophets and the Psalms must be fulfilled.” 24:46 and said to them, “Thus it is written, that the Christ should suffer and on the third day rise from the dead, 24:47 and that repentance and forgiveness of sins should be proclaimed in his name to all nations, beginning from Jerusalem.”

## They saw him

The two disciples on the road to Emmaus saw the glorified Christ. Luke 24:31 And their eyes were opened, and they recognized him. And he vanished from their sight.

## They told

These disciples told the others about the resurrection. Luke 24:35 Then they told what had happened on the road, and how he was known to them in the breaking of the bread.

## Christ brought peace

To remember the resurrection of Christ is to bring peace to the heart, not trouble. Luke 24:36 …… Jesus himself stood among them, and said to them, “Peace to you!” 24:38 And he said to them, “Why are you troubled, and why do doubts arise in your hearts?”

## Christ has been glorified

His sufferings were temporary, to bring abundant life to those who believed in HIM. The Scriptures reveal Christ, his attributes and his perfect work. As disciples of Christ, let us go about telling others of him. Holy Week is for walking and giving glory to the glorified Christ. Amen.
