A gift for thirty-two children was our goal, but God gave us more than we expected. The generous help of many brothers and friends arrived in time for my wife Celia to make the trip, accompanied by sister Juana M. and myself, to deliver the gifts to the children of the church in Huaracco.

[[foto1]]

It was a lightning trip of nine hours. The first vehicle took us to Santo Tomás, where we rested before continuing the next day to the community of Huaracco. When we arrived, brother Valerio Chávez gathered the children, and we found twenty-three more children who were not on the list. I cannot describe the faces of the children who had no gifts. What can we do for them? We made another list.

## Testimony

Since August of last year we had been praying over the names of the children of Huaracco. By November there were twenty children with their gift boxes. We shared this list of children with many brothers in different parts of Peru. One brother, Saúl B., shared the list with his coworkers (who are not believers), and a lady took an interest in it. The lady, named Ivy G., asked for the list, and a few days later she gave gift boxes for ten children. God gave more than we expected. He used many of our brothers from different parts of Peru, and even a person we do not know, but He does, and He moved her heart to fulfill His purposes. "And God gave Daniel favor and compassion in the sight of the chief of the eunuchs." (Daniel 1:9) The prophet Daniel knew this truth: it is God who grants favor.

The happy face of Moisés, the boy in the color picture, is a reward for all who gave for them. The apostle Paul said, quoting the words of the Lord Jesus Christ: it is more blessed to give than to receive.
