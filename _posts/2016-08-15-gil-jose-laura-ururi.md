GIL JOSÉ LAURA URURI

We praise God for the life of brother José. Every time we visit Desaguadero to train pastors and leaders, we find brother José with a desire to grow in his faith and the joy that marks him. He and his wife serve in the locality of Totoroma.

They received the courses given at the “Alfa y Omega” Baptist church in Desaguadero, Puno. His training began in 2012, and now we see him involved in the work, leading and serving the Lord in the church of Totoroma. This is a good testimony for continuing to invest our treasures and time in the lives of men and women like José and his wife.

In the EPYL ministry we keep striving to be useful, serving our great Master, the Lord Jesus Christ. Our request and petition is on behalf of him and his family. We ask that you help them with your prayers:

1. Strength in his faith and service.

2. Provision for the support of his family.

3. Provision for building the place where the church of Totoroma meets.

4. The health of brother José and his wife.

EPYL Ministry
