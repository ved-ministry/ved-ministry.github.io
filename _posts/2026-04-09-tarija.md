Ever since we began training in Desaguadero, Puno, it was our desire to take the training program to our brothers in the churches of Bolivia as well. God allowed us to make contact with David Yanapa, a Peruvian missionary sent from Juliaca. David is married to a Bolivian, and together they are raising up a church in the Los Trigales neighborhood of Tarija. I thank God for the company of Pastor Isaú Mucho, from Yunguyo; without his help and company I could not have reached Tarija. The trip took forty-six hours by road. God let us see what He is doing through the men He called to His work: many churches preaching the gospel of Christ. Amen. Pastor David showed us two churches and the work he is beginning.

## Evidence of the worldwide flood

[[foto2]]

During my trip to our sister country Bolivia, the whole way I could see the immense plateau and rocky valleys, different in character from Peru. Bolivia holds much evidence of a cataclysm, the flood. Kilometer after kilometer of dry, rocky mountains, as if placed there on purpose for man to look at. Thanks be to God for leaving evidence of His power.

God had created the earth and placed man on it to manage and care for it for His glory. But man chose to disobey and was expelled from Eden.

Man's wickedness was continually in his heart, and God decided to punish it with a worldwide flood. But a man named Noah found grace in the eyes of God, and he, his family and the animals God brought would be saved in an ark that Noah built at God's command. "For behold, I will bring a flood of waters upon the earth to destroy all flesh in which is the breath of life under heaven. Everything that is on the earth shall die." (Genesis 6:17)

> And of every living thing of all flesh, you shall bring two of every sort into the ark to keep them alive with you. They shall be male and female. Of the birds according to their kinds, and of the animals according to their kinds, of every creeping thing of the ground, according to its kind, two of every sort shall come in to you to keep them alive. Genesis 6:19-20

## Plans to help the work in Tarija

[[foto3]]

A mission trip in 2027. In the third week of January, God willing, we will take a group to train Sunday School teachers and at the same time work in an evangelistic campaign with children and teenagers (Vacation Bible School).

Connecting missionary David Yanapa with churches in Peru that want to support him. Thanks be to God for His protection and care; it was a blessing to get to know the churches and believers in Bolivia. — Cristóbal Ch., serving the Master
