The sierra of Peru is beautiful. After a day of teaching we climbed the hill above Desaguadero: the town below, Lake Titicaca to the horizon, and the snow peaks behind it.

This is the land of the Aymara churches we serve. If your church is praying about a mission trip to Peru, come and see it with your own eyes.
