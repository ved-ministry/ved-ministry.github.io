> So teach us to number our days that we may get a heart of wisdom. Psalm 90:12

## Pastors and leaders of Curahuasi

Our days this year are coming to an end, and God wants us to be wise, using our time well to keep impacting others with His Word. Ely and Paola Vega came to help with the module "Old Testament Survey". Thanks to their work among us, the men of God received the whole counsel of God for five days. Studying the Old Testament can be tiring, but these men endured and valued the teaching. Ely taught seven to eight hours a day. Let us accompany these men in their preparation with our prayers.

Pastor Reynaldo took on the challenge of hosting, together with the church he pastors, Pisonaypata Baptist Church. He also put us up in his home; he and his wife looked after us with great affection.

Julián pastors the church in Ocjoruro. Since the first module he has come to class with great interest. Thanks be to God for his life and his desire to grow.

All these brothers want to learn, but because of work and other difficulties they sometimes make it to only two or three days of the modules.

## New challenges in teaching

[[foto5]]

Santo Tomás, Chumbivilcas: language is a challenge for all of us who teach here. The oldest have the culture of Quechua and always insist that we teach in their language. Yet there is a group of young people who are growing and seeing the need for trained workers. Learning about church history helped and motivated them to keep preaching sound doctrine.

The church Pastor Donato pastors is three hours from Santo Tomás, in the community of Chiluyo. Brother Donato is a faithful student: in the nine courses we have given so far, he has never missed one.

[[foto2]]

In March 2026 we will have the last training in Santo Tomás and, God willing, graduate a group of students. For three years we have been traveling to build up the faith of these brothers. May God let us see some of them involved in the work.

> And I was with you in weakness and in fear and much trembling, and my speech and my message were not in plausible words of wisdom, but in demonstration of the Spirit and of power, so that your faith might not rest in the wisdom of men but in the power of God. 1 Corinthians 2:3-5

## Ways to keep helping us

[[foto1]]

Beloved, we continue to pray that God would grant us a vehicle so we can go and live in the house. We also want to praise God for the lives of our children, who keep advancing in their university studies.

In the second week of December, God willing, I will move ahead with building the house. We have been able to save a little money, but it is not enough for what we want to do. We hope to get as far as our savings allow.

It is our desire that our pastor Julio Jaramillo and his wife can come to teach the last course and be a blessing. Pray that God provides their tickets, and that Getsemaní church can send them. Amen.

## Year-end services in different places

The Churata Arche family and my family are preparing Christmas presentations. We will travel to two places: Kcosñipata, at Alex Herrera's church, on the morning of Wednesday the 24th, and Patria on the night of the 23rd. There we will be singing for our relatives, who are Catholic. May God allow us to present the gospel in song.

God bless you. Your missionaries.
