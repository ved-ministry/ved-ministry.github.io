Chichina is a community belonging to Ccatcca-Quispicanchi. In this community is the Baptist church of Chichina.

The desire of our brothers and the church’s leaders is to have greater quality in their Christianity and effectiveness in their leadership.

On Monday we left Cusco at 4:00 a.m. and arrived in Ccatcca at 6:30 a.m., from where we set out in a small truck to Chichina, arriving at 9:00 a.m.

That night we were able to meet with the leaders and brothers of the church, to whom we presented the work we do with the EPYL MINISTRY. Our only purpose is to keep equipping and training “more servants”, so that the local church may have greater spiritual growth and ministry impact.

Evaluating our weaknesses for doing our work in this church and community, we find ourselves limited in various ways, which I will call “obstacles”.

First: “Quechua” is their mother tongue, and many in the church and community speak little Spanish; few understand 100% of what is taught to them in Spanish.

Second: The level of education of many brothers is only primary school, and some have almost none.

Third: Communication and transport in this community are limited. There is almost no transport to travel to the place, and the teachers will have to go by motorbike or on foot.

[[foto1]]

Having evaluated these obstacles, we asked ourselves: will we be useful, giving our time, treasures, gifts and talents to help our brothers of the church in Chichina? We began to pray and to look for teachers for the three trainings of 2014, and for God to give us wisdom.

Dear brothers and churches, we plead that you help us by praying, so that we may do this work in the power of God and not in our own ability and wisdom.

God bless you.

Note: My companion in the battle, on this trip to coordinate the courses, was brother Porfirio Huillca; I appreciate his interest in the EPYL ministry and his friendship.

Pastor: Cristóbal Churata Q.

Serving the Master in Cusco, Peru.
