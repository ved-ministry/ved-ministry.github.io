Sicuani is the capital of the province of Canchis, Cusco. In this city there are three Baptist churches, started by two churches from Cusco. In 2023 we held a training for the first time at Agua Abundante Baptist Church. This year we returned to Sicuani for another training and had thirteen students, pastors and brothers from the churches of Sicuani and Sangarará. Evaluating the service of the EPYL ministry to the churches, we see shortcomings we need to improve to be effective in our goals. Reasons to pray: to enroll students for future trainings so we can follow up effectively, to keep praying for each student, and to motivate the pastors in their preparation.

## Elders

The men who lead the churches of Sicuani and Sangarará are mostly elderly. That is why it is urgent for us to involve the youth and give them the backing they need as part of the change to a new generation of leaders. We see potential in several young people and long for God to call them to His work.

## 2025

Next year we will have two trainings and complete the ten modules of the EPYL program. March 10–14, Sangarará: "General survey of church history". July 28 – August 1, Sicuani: "Missions, evangelism, discipleship". When the program ends, we long to see more servants equipped for the ministry.
