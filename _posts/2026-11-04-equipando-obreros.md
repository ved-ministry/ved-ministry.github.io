We thank our Lord and Savior for sending us to the land of the Aymara, in Desaguadero, in the department of Puno. This year we began in February with the first training, and today we close the year with the fourth module. God has been faithful in providing pastors and teachers and bringing them to these places. We thank the churches and brothers for their generous gifts and offerings, which make it possible for each teacher to share the blessed Word of God. Four modules were taught this year, by pastors Elías Huamán, Walmer Letona and Juan Valderrama, and brother Edgar Oribe.

## About the trainings

Every course is meant to give students tools they can use in their own study and spiritual growth. But four days of study only allow us brushstrokes of each course. It is our prayer that God would lead some to a seminary, to be equipped and perfected with the vision of returning to their own places, so that the gospel keeps running to save and to build up the saints.

## About the students

In Desaguadero and the surrounding area there are many churches and works, but in these years the modules only reach believers aged forty and over. This generation is not passing the Word of God on to its children, and the churches have no young people. The youth are focused on their own goals, professional careers and earning money. Beloved, let us keep praying for a generation of young people who want to be godly and servants of their God.

> Finally, brothers, pray for us, that the word of the Lord may speed ahead and be honored, as happened among you. (2 Thessalonians 3:1)
