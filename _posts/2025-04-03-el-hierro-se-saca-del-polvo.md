## The heart of the mountains

On my trips to the different places where we hold trainings with the EPYL ministry, I observe how the mountains were raised up in the universal flood, which occurred as judgment and because of the corruption of the earth before God. Looking at these high mountains in the Cusco highlands, my thoughts turn to the biblical account, and I wonder: what mineral materials may there be in those immense mountains and valleys that man has not yet been able to extract? In the wisdom book called Job, God reveals how man descends to the deepest parts of the mountains to take out gold, silver, iron and copper (Job 28). Only man can discover these paths or roads, by the capacities he has, and God has given him this benefit for his good. No lion, no wild beast has reached those places; not even the falcon’s eye has been able to discover them! (Job 28:7,8)

## The descendants of Adam

Zillah also bore Tubal-cain, the forger of all implements of bronze and iron… Gen. 4:22 The descendants of Adam were skilled men, creators of marvelous works. The great inventors used this knowledge that the wonderful Creator put in the heart of man. To what end? To enjoy the work of their hands and so live every day under the sun.

The heart of the mountains, full of dust and precious stones, for building the best inventions and so showing that God gave man wisdom and science. There is no science without God; he is the heart of good science.

……… fill the earth and subdue it, and have dominion over the fish of the sea and over the birds of the heavens and over every living thing that moves on the earth. (Gen. 1:28) Man has God’s blessing to bring the earth under his dominion and so manage it. However, the selfishness of the human race is nullifying God’s desire for his creation. What is that desire? To manage wisely the goodness and riches the earth gives you; use it to create airplanes, cars, build buildings, places to teach the beauty and greatness of the Creator. What God wants is not being fulfilled, only what man wants. Is it wrong to extract the minerals from the earth?

## Why have remote peoples not discovered these riches?

The Bible says: my people are destroyed for lack of knowledge. I can say that the inhabitants are losing these blessings for lack of true knowledge. These peoples focus only on receiving some assistance from governments. They do not use their reasoning and the true knowledge that is within them. Another factor that does not help these populations is the lack of scientific study. Young people are motivated only to keep their cultural wealth, such as traditional festivals, rites and worship of the apus, through dances. Hours of study are lost to cultural focuses. There is no scientific focus in education. The works of God are wonderful; observe and tell of his glory.

— C.Ch.
