Pero el pueblo en su mayoría decidió caminar en sus propios caminos sin considerar a su creador. Jehoshaphat son of Asa took up the reign of the southern kingdom, Judah. He walked in the ways of the LORD and did not seek the Baals. During his reign he made the nation prosper with wisdom and fear of God.

The king asked to consult the LORD before going out to battle against Ramoth-gilead because he loved to do what was right. King Ahab of Israel said there was a prophet, but that he hated him because he never prophesied good, but always evil. A true prophet prophesied what the LORD told him; that is what he spoke. When Micaiah appeared before King Ahab he had to foretell that Israel would be scattered on the mountains like sheep without a shepherd.

The prophets always brought God’s truth to the people so that they would walk in the right ways. But the people, for the most part, decided to walk in their own ways without considering their Creator. Today pastors continually warn believers not to follow their own ways, nor the teachings of men who announce their own thoughts based on corrupted works and ideas that seek vainglory. Pulpits abound with words of wise men, but without the Words of the LORD. The words of the apostle Paul are true: 1 Tim. 6:5 constant friction among people who are corrupted in mind and deprived of the truth, imagining that godliness is a means of gain; withdraw from such.

The man of God must speak what is in the Scriptures, making use of correct interpretation, as God said; limit yourself to the text, sweat heavily extracting the precious nuggets from the vein called the Bible. I remember the words of a friend who, quoting a godly Bible teacher, said: cut the Bible until blood comes out of it. On one occasion I read from Charles Spurgeon, “The Bible is not the light of the world, it is the light of the Church. But the world does not read the Bible, the world reads Christians. You are the light of the world.”

When we began the EPYL ministry, we believed that our reason for being and existing would be the principle of raising up men and women who love the Bible and, as good disciples of the Lord, are godly students, skilled so that its pages light up their understanding and its light guides their thoughts, paths and relationships. What is my reward? Seeing these brothers encouraged to focus on the Scriptures, because only and solely through them will their lives grow in the grace and knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ, Amen.
