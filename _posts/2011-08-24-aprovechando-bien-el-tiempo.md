“Making the best use of the time, because the days are evil.” Ephesians 5:16

This group of pastors and leaders eagerly await the courses of the ministry “Bible Training Center for Pastors” and make the most of their time. One example I must mention is the schedule: from 6:30 in the morning to 5:30 in the afternoon with 10-minute breaks. WHAT AN EXPERIENCE for me, to teach all day! The study of church administration strengthened their vision and ministries so they can serve the Lord better in their churches. Thanks be to God for that.

Another important detail is the age of these brothers: from 20, the youngest, to 65, the oldest. What motivates them to train and to know more of the Word of God? Their desire for God and to keep growing in grace in order to continue the ministry that the older ones began 40 years ago in this area of La Convención, Quillabamba, Cusco.

[[foto1]]

Beside me are Rómulo and Francisco. Rómulo is a new believer who has continued without growing weary since the day he was converted to the Lord, and has attended the training without missing a single course. Rómulo says: I was converted to the Lord by reading my little New Testament; I understood that if I died I would not go to heaven because I am a sinner, so I gave my life to Him, and as far as I can I want to serve Him. Now this young man is preparing himself and serving the Lord in order to go on to seminary.

Francisco is a leader brother of the first church of Quillabamba. He and a group of leaders took on the leadership of the church because they have no pastor, and we ask for your prayers for this church. Every Friday they meet to pray for a pastor and for the ministries of the church, waiting for God’s answer.

If you know of a brother or pastor who meets the requirements and would like to pastor a church with vision and mission, please let us know so we can put you in touch with the church. You can also share this news on your blog or Facebook so you can help us in this work.

Thank you all for your prayers and pleas to the Almighty God, who knows and understands our needs.

Cristóbal Churata Q.

Serving the MASTER, Cusco, Peru
