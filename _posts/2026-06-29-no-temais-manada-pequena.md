> Fear not, little flock, for it is your Father's good pleasure to give you the kingdom.

Words of encouragement from the Lord are always refreshing, because we are of His flock and He cares for us.

The faithfulness of the believers in Puno is a great encouragement: they came to the training in spite of the economic crisis they are going through. The church where the trainings are held is in Desaguadero, on the border with Bolivia. Believers in this area depend on trade, and trade was interrupted for twenty days by the strike in Bolivia. This hurt their finances and also attendance at the module.

Still, I praise God for the faithfulness of the men and women who came for the leadership classes taught by our brother Edgar Oribe. The churches need faithful servant-leaders who can guide others by example. Edgar is a living example of this module: he has served as a deacon at Victoria Baptist Church in Trujillo for more than twenty years. Thank you for your faithfulness, beloved brother; may the God we serve bless your life and your family.

## Reasons to pray for the believers and churches of Puno-Desaguadero

For the leadership of Alfa y Omega Baptist Church, that God would give them wisdom to plan together and develop the spiritual life of the believers. That the churches would learn to support their pastors with a salary, so the pastor's family can live trusting in God's provision. For believers who need to be obedient to the Father who saved them, that He would fill them with the knowledge of His will in all wisdom and that they would live in the power that comes from the Holy Spirit.

Beloved, we are grateful for your partnership in doing the work alongside us, with offerings that are a fragrant aroma, so we can keep equipping servants for the work of ministry. After three years serving God through the trainings, He himself is now leading us to begin a new church in Cusco. On August 15 and 16 we will officially open the new work; our pastor, Julio Jaramillo, will come to Cusco to preach during these two days of evangelism and edification.

> He is not here, for he has risen, as he said. Come, see (VED) the place where the Lord lay. Matthew 28:6
