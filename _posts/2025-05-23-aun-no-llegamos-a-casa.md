Morrison was a missionary in Africa in the late 1800s.

He and his wife had served on the mission field for 40 years. In fact, they never returned home until their last trip, which marked the end of their service in missionary work. As the ship approached the port of New York, they wondered whether anyone would still remember them. Would there be anyone there to receive them? This was before fax machines, cell phones and the internet existed, and they did not know whether anyone from the mission would be at the port when they arrived.

As the ship entered the harbor, Henry Morrison and his wife went up on deck and were completely surprised to see hundreds of people at the port with signs and banners saying “welcome.” There were balloons everywhere and people smiling and waving as the ship came closer and closer to land. Henry’s heart began to beat hard. Henry looked at his wife and said, “Dear, they have remembered us… they have come to welcome us.”

What they did not know was that in a private cabin, hidden from the rest of the passengers, Theodore Roosevelt himself was traveling, returning from a vacation in Africa. The signs were for him. The greetings and smiling friends were there for the President of the United States. Henry Morrison and his wife were not allowed to disembark until the president and his whole delegation left the ship first. Even the Marine Corps band was there to receive the president.

Henry Morrison said: “It simply does not seem fair. We have been serving the Lord faithfully for these 40 years. We have served in anonymity, but we have been faithful to God. Theodore Roosevelt comes from Africa after taking a vacation and everyone comes to welcome him. It does not seem right to me that we come home and there is no one to welcome us.” His wife looked at him and gave a great answer. She said, “But Henry, we are not home yet. We have not yet arrived at our true home.”

An author wrote, “If we were given everything we wanted here on earth, our hearts would settle for this world. God is always drawing us toward himself and his kingdom, where we will certainly find what we have always desired. Our home is not here – it is there. How do we get from here to there? Thanks to Our Redeemer – the Messiah – Jesus Christ.”

Taken from the sermon “Wisdom for the Heart – Job,” episode 15
