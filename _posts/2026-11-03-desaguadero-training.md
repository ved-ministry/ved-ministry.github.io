The EPYL ministry keeps equipping Aymara servants. Our vision is simple: to draw believers to the Word of God. These brothers and sisters long to grow and to be transformed into the image of Christ.

At Alfa y Omega church in Desaguadero, Puno, Pastor Juan Valderrama taught through the book of Romans in a simple and practical way, so that believers may live loving God.

Thank you for praying for the Aymara churches of the highlands.
