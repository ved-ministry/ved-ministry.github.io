To our fellow workers and friends

We thank the saints of the “New Jerusalem” Baptist Church in Totoray, Curahuasi.

God allowed us this year to begin equipping the pastors and leaders of this church. We finished the third course, “New Testament Survey”, this week, and we are preparing for the next modules. We thank Pastor Vaylete Velásquez Caqui, guest teacher for this module, for his help. Pastor Vaylete serves in Ebenezer Baptist Church in Trujillo.

The man of God in charge of this church is Pastor Grimaldo Ojeda; he and his family serve in this church.

[[foto2]]

During this year we were able to carry out three modules with the help of different servants of God who came to be a blessing to these brothers.

Prayer requests:

For the involvement in the work of these men who are growing in their faith, that they may be useful in God’s hands for the extension of his kingdom.

For the support of our guest teachers. Each of them is a great help. The Lord puts in their hearts the desire to equip the saints with us, knowing that the reward comes from above.

For wisdom for those of us at the head of this ministry. May God protect us in our service, because there are men who do not love the work of God.

Brothers, pray for us.

1 Thessalonians 5:25
