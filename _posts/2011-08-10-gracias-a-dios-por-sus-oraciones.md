Who is man? Where did he come from? “The cosmos is all that is, or ever was, or ever will be” (Carl Sagan, Cosmos, 1980, p. 4).

If we begin with man’s own thinking as the basis of our existence, we will be accepting evolution. At the youth camp in Pata Ccamara, Quispicanchis, Cusco, this topic was important for them because many are being bombarded in their faith.

While I was teaching, a young man was listening outside the room. When the talk was over he told me that he did not believe in a creator and that he had evolved. I asked him: how do you know that? Did you see the change from a monkey to a human being? Were you there? Were your teachers there? Evolutionists are increasingly bold, and openly deny and preach their ideas in the schools.

This week I travel to Quillabamba to teach a group of pastors and leaders, and I ask for your prayers.
