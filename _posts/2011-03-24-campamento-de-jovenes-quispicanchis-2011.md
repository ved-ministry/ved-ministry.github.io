Invitation letter to take part in the Quispicanchis youth camp

“Ausangate Expedition” 2011.

Dear brothers in Christ Jesus,

It is a pleasure to write to you with this letter, to invite you to be an instrument of God in the following ways:

1. Prayer for:

- The work team (directors, preachers, pastors, counselors and campers).

- The care and health of everyone.

- The goals and objectives we wish to accomplish.

- Material and spiritual needs.

2. Sending your young people (ages 14–30).

3. Giving:

- In products such as potatoes, sugar, rice, chicken and more.

- Financially (the 2011 contact details are omitted).

In past years this camp lasted three days, but starting this year we plan to hold it over one week, with an expedition to the snow-capped Ausangate, for which we ask for your spiritual and material support.

Oh, brothers, we honor your interest in this work. May God bless you in his divine grace.

… The Camp Committee.
