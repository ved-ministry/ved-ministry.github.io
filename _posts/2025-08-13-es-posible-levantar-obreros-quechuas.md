## The trainings: ten modules completed

For almost two years we have been training disciples in Chumbivilcas, and I see what God is doing in the lives of the young couples and the young people. The different teachers who came to teach the modules have been a great blessing. These teachers not only help the students grow intellectually; they also help the disciples in their spiritual maturity. Thanks to the following pastors: Enrique Hanco, Humberto Núñez, Elías Huamán, José Luis Murga, Américo Díaz, Julio Jaramillo, Héctor Chávez and Eduardo Alfaro. Behind these trained men and teachers there are many churches, families and brothers praying that God would keep impacting men and women with His Word, so that they can say, "Here I am, Lord, send me," because the fields are white for harvest. With much pain and tears in my eyes I write this part…

## About the church in Santo Tomás — Emanuel

The difficulties and trials are not improving. The situation of the past has still not been overcome; there is still opposition, and the enemy of our Lord seems to be gaining ground. Beloved, I ask that in the intimacy of your time with God you join our prayers, asking that our God and Lord would remove these men who keep harming the church; the lives and relationships of the church are being damaged. Amen. Pray that God would cleanse His church.

## Is it possible to raise up Quechua workers?

The Lord Jesus Christ called untrained men, and He trained them. That passion of our Lord drives us to do this work. We believe that being close to the believers and living alongside them, listening to their struggles in order to lead them to the Lord's thoughts and what He said in His Word, is the way to help them grow spiritually. In Christ there is consolation and perfection. Yes, of course it is possible to raise up Quechua workers in the context in which they live. Amen.

## Praises

For the lives of the teachers who helped us in a way we cannot explain. They gave their time, covered their own fares and expenses, and never asked us for anything in return. May God add to them and bless them in everything. For the churches that sustain us with their prayers and offerings: your service to the saints will be your reward. For my family, who accompany me in this precious, delicate, demanding and sacrificial work. I love you; you are my greatest treasures.

Three and a half years of going to these churches taught us to love them and to help with what was lacking in their faith. How precious it is to see lives challenged and trained to be used in the hands of our God, whose coming we await.
