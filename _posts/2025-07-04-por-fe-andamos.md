God was good to provide fares, lodging and everything for this trip to Sullana, Piura, for the AMEBIP congress, where I was able to meet many pastors and missionaries involved in spreading the gospel in different parts of Peru.

A note: passing through Lima I was able to visit our church, Getsemaní in Santa Anita. I also visited Promesa church in Chaclacayo, with whom we coordinated the mission trip for the Vacation Bible School in Sicuani in January 2026. I was able to see our children, Caleb and Cesia, who serve at FE Baptist Church in Surco.

Now we are preparing for a month of intense activity with three trainings. Early Sunday morning we travel to Curahuasi with Pastor Juan Valderrama to teach the module "Bible study methods and rules of interpretation". From Sunday on there will be much activity and teaching. Pray for us.

The Lord knows our passion for delivering the message and the truth to our beloved Quechua fellow servants, just as He knows our financial scarcity. Every training demands a good investment, for fares, materials and more. I have always believed and seen the hand of God in my life: He has met every need, small or great, and has never left us alone. (For we walk by faith, not by sight, 2 Corinthians 5:7.) He has sustained the work we do. Even when we were working to sow the gospel and begin the VEN-ID church, God showed us His goodness and mercy. May our faith not diminish but rather exalt the name of God Almighty. Amen.

Oh Lord, take us to be a blessing and strength to your children; may the truth of your Word transform the lives and thoughts of your people. "And also for me, that words may be given to me in opening my mouth boldly to proclaim the mystery of the gospel." (Ephesians 6:19)

I thank God for missionaries Porfirio Huillca and Roberto Choque, who serve in different places: Porfirio in Lamay, Calca, and Roberto in Chiclayo, at Betel Baptist Church. Both are good friends and fellow workers.
