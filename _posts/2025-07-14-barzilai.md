Los hombres de bien, siempre serán recordados por otros. Aristarco, mi compañero de prisiones, Jesús, llamado Justo; que son los únicos de la circuncisión que me ayudan en el reino de Dios, y han sido para mí un consuelo. Lucas el médico amado. Col. 4:7-14\n\nEn mis viajes a los lugares de capacitaciones, muchos hombres me ayudan para llegar a esos lugares. Mi labor ahora es conectar a otros misioneros con iglesias, para que así la misericordia de Dios siga obrando para bien de otros. En mi viaje a Taratara, observe a un pastor que tenía su Biblia bien usada, las hojas se desprendían. Tome una foto, y lo publique en mi estado de WhatsApp, inmediatamente un hermano lo vio y decidió hacer llegar una ofrenda para poder proveer una Biblia nueva al pastor Julián. Nuestro servicio, es a los santos, y así manifestar su misericordia a otros. But deal loyally with the sons of Barzillai the Gileadite, and let them be among those who eat at your table, for with such loyalty they met me when I fled from Absalom your brother. (1 Kings 2:7) Who was Barzillai? A Gileadite eighty years old, who came to receive King David and cross the Jordan to enter Jerusalem. Barzillai was a very rich man, and he declined to go with the king to Jerusalem because he was already old. 19:33 And the king said to Barzillai, “Come over with me, and I will provide for you with me in Jerusalem.” 19:34 But Barzillai said to the king, “How many years have I still to live, that I should go up with the king to Jerusalem? 19:35 I am this day eighty years old.

Can I discern between what is pleasant and what is not? Can your servant taste what he eats or what he drinks? Can I still listen to the voice of singing men and singing women? Why then should your servant be an added burden to my lord the king?” 2 Samuel 19:33-35. Barzillai sustained and gave provisions to King David and his army when he was at Mahanaim. In addition, Barzillai declined to go and be sustained by King David in Jerusalem, because of his old age and above all his modesty and humility. Unlike others, Barzillai decided not to take advantage of the king’s friendship and regard.

Later, near his death, King David remembers Barzillai and his descendants, commanding his son Solomon to show kindness and sustain them. Why did King David remember Barzillai before his death? Good men will always be remembered by others. The apostle Paul remembered the many men who did him good, above all those who helped him in the ministry; he prayed and interceded for them, that others might do them good. In the letter to the Colossians he lists a group of men and their qualities. Tychicus, a beloved brother and faithful minister and fellow servant in the Lord, Onesimus, our faithful and beloved brother, who is one of you. Aristarchus, my fellow prisoner, and Jesus who is called Justus. These are the only men of the circumcision among my fellow workers for the kingdom of God, and they have been a comfort to me. Epaphras, who is one of you, a servant of Christ Jesus, always struggling on your behalf in his prayers, that you may stand mature and fully assured in all the will of God. Luke the beloved physician. Col. 4:7-14

On my trips to the places of training, many men help me to reach those places. I have seen God working and doing good through them; I am a blessed man to have many collaborators. My work now is to connect other missionaries with churches, so that God’s mercy keeps working for the good of others. On my trip to Taratara, I saw a pastor whose Bible was well worn, its pages coming loose. I took a photo and posted it on my WhatsApp status; immediately a brother saw it and decided to send an offering so that a new Bible could be provided for Pastor Julián. Our service is to the saints, and so to show his mercy to others. But by the grace of God I am what I am, and his grace toward me was not in vain. On the contrary, I worked harder than any of them, though it was not I, but the grace of God that is with me. 1 Cor. 15:10
