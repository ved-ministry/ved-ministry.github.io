Equipping and strengthening the lives of pastors and leaders is the vision of the ministry that was born in 2012. That year we began the ministry under the backing of Ven-Id Baptist Church in two departments: Cusco and Puno-Desaguadero. It is a great joy for us to see the brothers of the churches grow and bring their ministries to life.

EMILIANA MENDOZA ALARCÓN, ANGÉLICA MENDOZA MENDOZA, WENDY ALICIA CAHUAYA PASCAYA, DULIO DANTE MILLARES MENDOZA, ANDRÉS QUIÑONES JIHUAÑA, LUIS FERMÍN CONDORI CARITA, GIL JOSÉ LAURA URURI, JULIO CÉSAR LARAMANI LIMACHI.
