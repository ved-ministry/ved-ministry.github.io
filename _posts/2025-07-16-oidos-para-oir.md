> If anyone has ears to hear, let him hear. Mark 4:23

When Jesus was by the sea, a great crowd gathered to hear his teaching. Jesus told the parable of the sower, and at the end he said to them: He who has ears to hear, let him hear. The people who heard the parable did not understand its meaning. The Lord told them to think about what they had heard. But those who were close to HIM, and the disciples, wanted to know the meaning of the parable, and asked him about it. Christ explained the parable and added another teaching about the light. What did the disciples need?

Christ wanted his close followers, the disciples, to understand and meditate on the teaching he gave. The brothers in the churches are good hearers. I learned that they are good at retaining and grasping the teachings of the Scriptures. Although they are not skilled at observation and reading, because there is no culture of reading, they are nevertheless very good at hearing.

In several places where we train, again and again I hear from them statements like these. What I heard, now I understand it. With words of self-criticism they say: because I did not take time to read and learn the Scriptures. Others say: I should have given more time to learning the Word so as to know him well. Generally, these statements come from the older brothers. The Lord Jesus continues to tell us: he who has ears to hear, let him hear.
