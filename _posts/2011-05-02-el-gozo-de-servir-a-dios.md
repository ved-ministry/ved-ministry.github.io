Pastor Felipe, of the church in Ccatcca. This brother has dedicated his life to the ministry in that area. Pastor Felipe has many occupations: he works as a bricklayer, and does other jobs to support his family. But he does it with joy and love, because he knows that one day the Lord’s rewards for him will be many.
