God has planned in his grace for us to be part of, and a help to, the pastors and churches of the Ccatcca area this year. On Wednesday the 23rd we had our first conversation and work planning for 2011, with four pastors and two leaders taking part.

1. TRAINING OF PASTORS AND LEADERS AT THE EVANGELICAL BAPTIST CHURCH OF CCATCCAPAMPA, April 28, 29 and 30.

2. INVITATION TO THE PASTORS’ TRAINING AT VEN-ID BAPTIST CHURCH, June 1, 2 and 3.

3. QUISPICANCHIS YOUTH CAMP, “Ausangate Expedition” 2011.

In these trainings I will have the help of brother Porfirio Huillca, who was my classmate at the Baptist Seminary of Peru.

I am grateful to God for my brothers in Ccatcca and for their great interest and desire to grow until they become like the image of the Lord Jesus Christ. In this meeting each one voiced his concern about how little the Word of God is being taught. For me it is a challenge and a great opportunity to be useful to them out of love for the Lord.

I ask for your prayers and supplications.

In Christ,

Cristóbal Churata Q.

Serving the Master in Cusco
