Beloved pastors and leaders,

Greetings and blessings in the precious name of our Lord Jesus Christ, whom we worship and serve.

We are about to complete six years, and the Ven-Id church has set its heart on holding the Bible Conferences “The Life of Jesus Christ”, in order to keep “growing in the grace of God”.

For this occasion our guest speaker will be Pastor Julio Jaramillo,

of Ebenezer Baptist Church in Trujillo and teacher at the Baptist Seminary of Peru.

The classes will be on June 1, 2 and 3.

Cost: S/. 5.00 for pastors and

S/. 10.00 for leaders and brothers. Includes meals and materials.
