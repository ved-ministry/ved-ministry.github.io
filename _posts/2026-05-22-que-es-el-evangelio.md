1. It is the teaching of Jesus. Jesus went about all the cities and villages, teaching in their synagogues and proclaiming the GOSPEL of the kingdom and healing every disease and every affliction among the people. Matthew 9:35

2. It is the power of God for salvation. For I am not ashamed of the gospel, for it is the power of God for SALVATION to everyone who believes, to the Jew first and also to the Greek. Romans 1:16

3. The good news of God. So with many other exhortations he preached the GOOD NEWS to the people. Luke 3:18 And he came and preached the GOOD NEWS of peace to you who were far off and peace to those who were near. Ephesians 2:17

## What will you do with the gospel?

But to all who did receive him, who believed in his name, he gave the right to become children of God. John 1:12
