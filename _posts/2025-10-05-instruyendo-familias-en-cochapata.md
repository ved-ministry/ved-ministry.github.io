Miguel and Elsa Chino work with 180 children in Toccoccori and Cochapata, together with a team of brothers from the churches there. They hold a mini-camp for the parents of those children. Many of the parents are unbelievers. Miguel and Elsa's desire is for the parents of those children to know Christ. That is why they hold a two-day camp for the parents, in which the believers of the Cochapata church take part.

[[foto1]]

During those two days I taught these families at the church in Cochapata. The teaching was about the family. We were also able to present the gospel to couples who come from Catholicism. The Word of God is living and active and can make the simple wise. Thanks be to God for the believers of that area, who are giving testimony and working so that the people of their place see the works of God in their lives. Pray for Miguel and Elsa.

[[foto2]]

For our next trainings: Curahuasi, October 27–31; Chumbivilcas, November 3–7.
