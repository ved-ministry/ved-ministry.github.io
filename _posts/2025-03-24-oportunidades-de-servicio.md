Our service to churches in different places continues to be encouraging. Yesterday, Sunday, we went to Arín, Huarán, to teach the married couples at Vida Abundante church. Pastor Saúl Sangama asked us to teach three sessions on the family. We saw several young couples who are at the stage of raising children. The church has a ministry for families that meets every month to grow and have fellowship. Praise God.

We finished the three trainings that began in February in Curahuasi and continued in March in Chumbivilcas and Sangarará. Now we look ahead to July and August for the next three. I ask your prayers for this group of young people from Chumbivilcas, that God would keep calling workers to His harvest, and for the families of Vida Abundante church in Arín, Huarán.
