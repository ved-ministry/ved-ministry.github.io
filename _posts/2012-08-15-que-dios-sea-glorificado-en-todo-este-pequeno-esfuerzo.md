REPORT ON THE SECOND TRAINING MODULE “OLD TESTAMENT SURVEY”

FIRST: THE JOURNEY

I left Cusco on Sunday, July 29, 2012 at 10:00 p.m., arriving in Puno at 6:00 a.m. the next day. I immediately took the bus to Desaguadero, arriving at 9:30 a.m., where Pastor Ascencio Sarmiento and his family received me with a warm welcome and a restoring breakfast.

SECOND: THE OPENING OF THE MODULE

The second module was opened the same day at 10:00 a.m. at the “Alfa y Omega” Baptist Church (where the whole module took place). We opened the module with more than 25 students, among them pastors, leaders, Sunday School teachers and brothers from the “Alfa y Omega” church.

THIRD: THE DEVELOPMENT

The module ran normally from Monday to Saturday, suspending Friday because of the town’s commercial activity. We used an average of 9 hours of study each day.

On both Wednesday and the last Sunday before returning, at the service, I was invited to preach at the church.

During the week I was the object of our Lord’s blessing, being blessed, guided, encouraged, motivated, overpraised and overfed by the gentle (very kind and generous brothers of the church), who tried to supply all, all of my needs.

[[foto1]]

My gratitude to Pastor Ascencio Sarmiento and his family, who attended to my needs.

To the family of brother Basilio, who together with Pastor Ascencio prepared the place where I rested comfortably and peacefully.

To brother Domingo, who with his wife shared their blessings with me.

To the sisters of the church who cooked and tried to make me gain weight. To every brother and sister who made the effort to listen to me during the week. To the pastors who came daily to receive the module, even in the intense cold.

[[foto2]]

FOURTH: THE CLOSING

We closed the module on Saturday, July 4, at 4:30 in the afternoon, with an average of 25 permanent students. The total number enrolled was 36 students.

FIFTH: THE BOOKS

5 books were missing for the same number of permanent students, who completed more than 40 hours. The secretary who took attendance will send a record of attendance, so that the missing books can be sent.

I am extremely grateful to God for allowing me to travel to Desaguadero and share the little I have been able to learn of his Word with the brothers there. It has been a very great blessing to me.

May God be glorified in all this small effort made with the module.

Humberto Núñez Trujillo

EPYL ministry
