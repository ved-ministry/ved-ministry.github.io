The work in Salvación is blessed to have a missionary who is investing his life and resources for the advance of the Word of God. Richar Espinoza Caldas and his family left their relatives and their city, Huánuco, to serve the Lord in Salvación, Manu, Madre de Dios. This family started a small chicken-farming business, investing much money and time, to support the work and the family's needs.

[[foto1]]

Often the missionary has to do all the work: leading, teaching and organizing the church services. The journey to Salvación from Cusco can be difficult. The rains swept away several kilometers of the road, and this left the population without food supplies. We are praying for: strength and encouragement for the missionary family; growth and maturity for the believers, that they would get involved in serving God; care and protection for the missionary's wife, Yesenia, that God would keep her in her pregnancy.

[[foto2]]

And for us, that we would live open-handed and give more to the service.
