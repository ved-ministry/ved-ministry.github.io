A brother titled the book of Colossians “the mystery of Christ in the believer”, and it is indeed a mystery how our Lord dwells in and changes the life of a man or woman to make them like his image. The title I gave this book is “the fruitful life in Christ”. Is it possible for the believer to be fruitful in his walk, his actions and his thinking? Of course it is! The book is a letter full of basic principles for the full knowledge of God’s will. This letter changed my life and my thinking about God and his will. How is it possible that Christ became man to save me and reconcile me with God the Father? Now that I am saved, what can I do with my life? The letter explains God’s will for your life.

Verse 9 of chapter one is encouraging and motivating, within Paul’s personal prayer for the Colossians: “And so, from the day we heard, we have not ceased to pray for you, asking that you may be filled with the knowledge of his will in all spiritual wisdom and understanding” (Col. 1:9). Like the apostle Paul, it is our prayer for the brothers, groups and churches where we are training, educating, motivating, exhorting and discipling, that they may be filled with the knowledge of his will in spiritual wisdom and understanding.

Yet we are aware of the tremendous responsibility and privilege entrusted to the EPYL ministry, and so we also ask for your help through your prayers.

Prayer requests

1. The spiritual life of the team members (teachers).

2. Teamwork with the churches and areas.

3. Provision for the students and teachers.

Mobilizing and sending

1. Our brother Enrique Hanco traveled to two areas to train on different dates. The first training took place at the “IEBIM” Baptist church in Calca, where the course “Bible Study Methods” has not yet been completed, so he will have to go back. This demands time and money. We ask for your prayers.

The second time Enrique traveled was to Ccatcca, to train 20 brothers, three of whom were pastors. We began on Monday and finished on Friday at midday with a lunch. In the evenings Enrique preached at the services to the brothers of the Ccatccapampa church.

2. On the night of Monday the 12th we began the course “Old Testament Survey” at our church building, and the teacher is our brother Florencio Churata. Five students attend this course, four from our church and one from the First Church of Cusco.

3. On Tuesday the 13th the course “New Testament Survey” began with six students attending, and Roberto Choque teaches these classes. Roberto and his family are members of our church, working in the Sunday School ministry.

4. Friday the 16th, opening of the course “Bible Study Methods”.

These classes will be taught in the evenings for four months, and with God’s help we hope to finish with the same number of students.

WE ASK FOR YOUR PRAYERS

EPYL Ministry of Ven-Id Baptist Church
