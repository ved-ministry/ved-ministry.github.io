Greetings, fellow workers. This year we began our ministry work with Vacation Bible Schools. We connected three sister churches with three teams made up of believers from different churches of the same faith and practice. Pastor Duglas Serrudo received the brothers from La Promesa Baptist Church in Lima, led by Pastor Jonathan Dancourt Braithwaite. The believers of the Baptist church in Quellouno were encouraged by the work with children and the evening services.

Mission trips can create bonds of fellowship among believers. Now we understand the words of the apostle Paul to the Philippians: "I count everything as loss because of the surpassing worth of knowing Christ Jesus my Lord… not having a righteousness of my own that comes from the law, but that which comes through faith in Christ." (Philippians 3:8-9)

The work in Quellouno right now needs a meeting place. While the evening services were going on, the land was already sliding, and we had to hold the service in the street. The church's building had wooden posts and roof and was enclosed with burlap. Now they have no building, and the rains are still heavy.

If God allows you and your family, after giving to your local church, I encourage you to give to the work in Quellouno. The site is still in rubble, and the believers are meeting in homes. If you would like to help the missionary, write to us and we will put you in touch.

## Our journey continues

On Monday we leave for Curahuasi, where we will begin training the pastors and leaders of the churches. Pray for us, that we teach the Word of God well.
