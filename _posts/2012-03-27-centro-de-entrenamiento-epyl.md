EQUIPPING PASTORS AND LEADERS MINISTRY – CUSCO, PERU

LOCATION: the training zones of the EPYL ministry.

The first training program is at our church building: Ven-Id Baptist Church, Urb. Túpac Amaru, Jr. Antonio Bastidas Q 1-4. The courses are taught in the evenings on Mondays, Tuesdays and Fridays.

The second zone is in Quispicanchis – Ccatcca. We have 20 people, of whom three are senior pastors, one a co-pastor, and the rest are leaders or aspiring leaders.

The third zone is in Desaguadero, Puno (on the border with Bolivia). The date scheduled for the first course is April 9 to 14.

The fourth zone is in the Sacred Valley of the Incas, Calca.

BACKGROUND: The first course was taught in Calca, to leaders. The courses are in a module format over a whole week, three times a year, completing the whole program in three and a half years. The second group, Quispicanchis, received the first course on March 5 to 9, and so on with the following groups and zones.

COURSE CURRICULUM:

[[foto1]]

Course 1. Bible Study Methods and Rules of Interpretation

Course 2. Old Testament Survey

Course 3. New Testament Survey

Course 4. Preaching Biblical Messages and Pastoral Ministry

Course 5. Survey of Biblical Doctrine

Course 6. Spiritual Life

Course 7. Church Ministry, Administration and Education

Course 8. Principles and Methods of Teaching

Course 9. Survey of Church History

Course 10. Missions, Evangelism and Discipleship

REPORT ON FINANCES: Each course costs S/. 20.00. If you would like to support a national pastor, please contact the EPYL ministry or Pastor Cristóbal Churata Quispe by e-mail: churataquispe@yahoo.com.

PRINCIPLES OF THE EPYL MINISTRY

The members of the EPYL center are mature believers prepared to give practical teaching to the students.

We hold the principle of giving our time, money and gifts to the ministry without expecting financial payment from it.

PURPOSES OF THE EPYL MINISTRY

The EPYL program was conceived as a ministry to train and equip “within the church”; it is not a formal education institution.

The EPYL MINISTRY is a practical ministry for training and equipping. The goal is to impact the local church in terms of spiritual growth, through the equipping and training of its pastors and leaders.
