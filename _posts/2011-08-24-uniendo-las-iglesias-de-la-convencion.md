The Nazareno Baptist Church of Cirialo was the place of the training, five hours from Quillabamba. In the evenings I preached to the brothers and those who came, calling them to have a vision for serving the Lord despite their circumstances. The area is agricultural, and families depend on coffee and cocoa for their living.

Thanks to the ministry “Bible Training Center for Pastors”, the pastors and leaders are coming together more for the work of the churches. Now they await the next course, in December, the 5th to the 9th, in Palma Real.

[[foto1]]

Palma Real is the church where brother Víctor works. He has never attended a Bible seminary, but he does want to know the Lord and his Word better. With the help of some pastors and written materials, he has been preparing himself to help his local church, where he attends with his family.

His greatest concern is for the families of the church and of the town where the church is. Sins and problems grow worse for marriages because of a poor understanding of biblical principles and the poor examples they have to follow. In this place there are broken marriages, abandoned mothers with four children, children without fathers, and every kind of licentiousness. When they come to the church the work is difficult for the pastors, because there are other sins and other people involved in each case. May God have mercy and grace on them and on us, and may we reflect on the care of the families God has given us.

Cristóbal Churata Q.

Serving the MASTER in Cusco, Peru
