The EPYL ministry keeps equipping Aymara servants. Our vision is to draw believers to the Word of God. These brothers and sisters long to grow and to be transformed into the image of Christ.

From November 2 to 5, twenty believers gathered at Alfa y Omega church in Desaguadero, Puno. Pastor Juan Valderrama, from La Esperanza church in Trujillo, taught through the whole book of Romans in a simple and practical way, so that believers may live loving God.

> “It has changed the way I see things, and what God requires of me.” — Brother Basilio Escobar

**Pray with us:** that God would strengthen the leadership of the churches in Desaguadero. They need a pastor.

**Next training:** February 22–25, 2027, in Desaguadero, studying the book of Revelation.
