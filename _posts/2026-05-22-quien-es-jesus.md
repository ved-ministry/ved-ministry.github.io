1. He is the Son of God. Simon Peter answered, “You are the Christ, THE SON OF THE LIVING GOD.” Matthew 16:16

2. He is God. Thomas answered him, “MY LORD AND MY GOD!” John 20:28 To them belong the patriarchs, and from them, according to the flesh, is the Christ, WHO IS GOD over all, blessed forever. Amen. Romans 9:5

3. He is Savior. For if while we were enemies we were reconciled to God by the death of his Son, much more, now that we are reconciled, WE SHALL BE SAVED BY HIS LIFE. Romans 5:10 For unto you is born this day in the city of David a Savior, who is CHRIST the Lord. LUKE 2:11

## What will you do with Jesus Christ?

But to all who did receive him, who believed in his name, he gave the right to become children of God. John 1:12
