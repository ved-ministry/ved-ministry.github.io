The EPYL program was conceived to train and equip "within the local church". EPYL is a practical ministry of training and equipping. The goal is to impact the local church in terms of spiritual growth by equipping and training its pastors and leaders. We focus on forming leaders in the likeness of the character of Jesus Christ.

We educate, train, motivate, disciple and exhort the disciples, with the goal of reproducing lasting leadership, through ten courses (modules) designed to be taught over one week each: to instruct pastors and church leaders in biblical knowledge and practical training for ministry, and to equip them to handle the Word of God rightly and fruitfully, whether in preaching, teaching or counseling.

Thanks be to God, the group of pastors and brothers in Curahuasi, Apurímac, want to grow and receive the modules. From July 7 to 11 the module "Methods and rules of interpretation" will be taught. We ask your prayers for Pastor Juan Valderrama, for provision and care.

We ask you to keep praying for the work God has entrusted to us. There is a group of young people in Chumbivilcas eager to keep growing, to serve the living and true God, and to be steadfast as they wait for the coming of Christ. From August 4 to 8, God willing, we will travel to train with Pastor Eduardo Alfaro. Pray that God uses the teacher and that the wisdom of God is imparted.

For us: to keep growing in our faith and love for Christ and His work. Care and wisdom for our children, who live in Lima. For edification and evangelism, whenever God gives us opportunities to speak of Him.
