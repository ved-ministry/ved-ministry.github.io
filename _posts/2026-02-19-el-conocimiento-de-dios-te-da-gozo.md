These days in the nation where I was born, there is a lot of political noise. While this was going on, I was busy helping a missionary in the work of teaching children the Word of God. I look at the children’s lives and wonder: what might these children be thinking about what is happening in our nation? Watching them, I notice that they only think about having fun and having a good time. The prophet Jeremiah constantly reminds me how God is in control of the nations. It cannot be overlooked that above the inhabitants and rulers, such as kings, princes and armies, God remains above them all. Jer. 32:27 Behold, I am the LORD, the God of all flesh; is anything too hard for me? – For the Lord nothing is impossible, and he remains in control. Thank you, because he remains in control of the governments and inhabitants of a nation.

For that reason I say there is joy in a believer who knows God, because he knows first-hand that a higher being remains in control of the nations and their rulers. Then, what can I do as a citizen of a nation? I exhort, above all, that supplications and prayers be made. Be subject for the Lord’s sake to every human institution, whether to the king as supreme, and wait eagerly for the coming of the day of God.
