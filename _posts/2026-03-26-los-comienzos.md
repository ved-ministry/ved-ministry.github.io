For three years we traveled through the provinces of Cusco training pastors and brothers who want to grow and know more of God. This work of preparation is something the local church and seminaries should be doing through their different ministries, in order to prepare workers for the harvest. It is also a good tool for members of the local church to see the fields white for harvest and the great urgency of sending missionaries. To evangelize, disciple and do missions is God's desire; the world continually goes after the desires of the flesh, and only the gospel can save it.

## Hunger to grow

In every place we reach with the trainings we have always seen a couple of disciples, or more, with the desire to grow and to know the One who loves them and saved them: God and His Son, our Lord. The method has not changed. Theological studies by extension or online are good, but they do not reach the disciple's primary need: fellowship and communion with the pastors and teachers who teach the subjects and sessions.

Believers in the small communities take a long time to open up; they do not easily give their trust to others. Because of their cultural context they are very reserved. Spending time with them is the key: listening to them and carrying their burdens and struggles encourages them greatly. There is hunger for the Word of God; they love our heavenly Father and want to do His will.

## From Genesis

[[foto1]]

We met a family with whom we are having Bible studies. I began teaching them the principles, the origins, from the book of Genesis, and I see in them such a desire to learn and devour the Word. The family has a daughter who is studying at university (UNSAAC). I see in her a hunger to learn: she has her Bible and her notebook, and then she asks questions.

God is leading the small group to begin a new work. The brother, head of the household, has expressed his desire to lend his home to gather people and so make more disciples. Your prayers for the family of Rolando and Alicia.

Beloved, keep praying for us. May the Lord keep building up your lives.

Blessings — Cristóbal Churata Q.
