We did not want to leave the children without blessings, so my wife Celia, with the help of our children, started the "Christmas boxes". Every child on the list of the Baptist church in Huaracco will now receive a gift box. This morning I received the list with twenty-five children already covered, but seven remained. I asked myself: what will we do for these seven children? Praying to the Lord, I told Him of this need.

## Others gave more

[[foto1]]

A brother who supports the EPYL ministry had told a coworker about the Christmas boxes, and she gave beyond what we expected. We received the news and rejoiced, remembering the name of our God. Thirty-two children were blessed, and we added four more. God always surprises us, using His children and also His creatures made in His image. I ask prayer for the salvation of Ivy del Carmen Guarnizo.

## By faith

Over the years God has always shown me that when I doubt, He shows me more than I ask. By faith we began the ministry Equipping Pastors and Leaders; we never had the full budget to cover the trainings, travel and other expenses. We finish the year, and all six trainings were carried out without any setback. Beloved fellow workers, thank you for praying and for being our support in prayer and fellowship at all times. Without you we could not move forward in this small work we do for the Quechua servants, whom we love unconditionally. Amen.

> For we walk by faith, not by sight. 2 Corinthians 5:7

The Churata Berna family — serving the Master in Cusco
