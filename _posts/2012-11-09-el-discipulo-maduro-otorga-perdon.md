It is my duty as a disciple of Christ to forgive my neighbor, in obedience to God.

The mature disciple is willing to build up with his words and deeds, because that is how he will be following the teachings of his Lord, who in love asked his followers in John 13:34: “A new commandment I give to you, that you love one another: just as I have loved you, you also are to love one another.”
