> Of them you may eat: the locust of any kind, the bald locust of any kind, the cricket of any kind, and the grasshopper of any kind. Leviticus 11:22 Now John wore a garment of camel’s hair and a leather belt around his waist, and his food was locusts and wild honey. Matthew 3:4

The food of John the Baptist was given by God himself, who in the law commanded the Hebrews which kinds of insects they could eat. Can you imagine one morning in the desert of Judea, John sitting down to enjoy a delicious breakfast?

## Fellowship

The Lord Jesus Christ is our model to imitate. He walked through all places to spread the gospel, drawing near to the poor, the blind, the lame, the maimed, tax collectors and sinners. In his example we see the way to do ministry, and we want to be faithful to him, spreading his Word to every creature. Amen

For 17 years we served in the planting of the Ven-Id Baptist church in Cusco, Peru. Today we serve pastors in rural areas in the following places: Cusco, Apurímac and Puno, with the ministry of equipping pastors and leaders.
