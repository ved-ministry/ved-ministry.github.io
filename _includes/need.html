Serving since 1997. Today they live in a single room.

Cristóbal and Celia have given their lives to the Quechua and Aymara churches of Peru. They travel by bus, sometimes for days, to bring the Word to pastors in the countryside. Meanwhile they live in one room: their house goes up little by little, as far as their savings reach, and they have no vehicle to get up to it. They are praying for transport.
